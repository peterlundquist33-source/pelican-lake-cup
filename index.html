Championship Weekend

How the Week Unfolds

Every Cup has followed the same rhythm since 2020: arrive, draft, eat, and then settle it over five rounds. The shape below is drawn from past Cups — the 2027 schedule is confirmed closer to the event.

Arrival

The Field Assembles

Players make their way north to the Brainerd Lakes. The cabin becomes tournament headquarters for the week — it has been the base of the Cup since the event outgrew a half-finished build and a camper in the yard.

Draft Night

Captains Select

Two captains, twenty names, and a self-scouting report from every player. In 2023 the draft was held outdoors at Breezy Point Airport with a cookout and lawn chairs. In 2024 it moved earlier so team shirts could be sized and ordered.

Opening Night

The Cinderella Boyz Cook

A tradition since 2021: the challengers handle the opening night dinner. Hospitality first, hostilities second.

Competition

Five Rounds, Five Formats

Scramble, two shambles, alternate shot, and singles match play. Each round is scored match-play style and every point carries to the cumulative scoreboard.

Evening

Off the Course

Lake time, a supper club dinner — Norway Ridge in 2021 — side games, and the running argument about who actually carried whom.

Sunday

The Cup Is Presented

Singles match play closes the week, the final points are counted, and the trophy goes home with somebody. For four straight Cups, that somebody has been the Top Guns.

The Battlefield

Deacon's Lodge

An Arnold Palmer Signature design laid across 150 acres of Minnesota's Brainerd Lakes country. Mixed forest, wetland, and the shoreline of Pelican Lake make it one of the most beautiful — and least forgiving — layouts in the state. It has hosted more Cup golf than any other course.

Competition

Five Rounds. Five Ways to Win.

Every format tests something different — teamwork, strategy, nerve, and individual grit. Points are awarded match-play style and aggregate across all five rounds. The team with the most total points wins the Cup.

1

2v2 Scramble

Both players hit, pick the best ball, repeat. The ultimate team format — lean on your partner's strengths.

Handicap
35% low + 15% high
2

2v2 Shamble — Combined Net

Both players hit the tee shot, pick the best drive, then each plays their own ball in. Combined net score counts.

Handicap
60% of course handicap
3

2v2 Shamble — Best Net

Same as Round 2 — pick the best drive, play your own ball — but only the best net score of the two partners counts each hole.

Handicap
85% of course handicap
4

Alternate Shot Scramble

Partners alternate shots on each hole. Pick the best tee shot first, then trade off from there. Communication is everything.

Handicap
35% of combined team handicap
5

Singles Match Play

Head to head. Hole by hole. No partner to bail you out. This is where legends are made.

Handicap
Full course handicap
—

Scoring & Tiebreaker

Each round is scored match-play style and results aggregate on the cumulative scoreboard. If the Cup is tied after five rounds, Round 5 serves as the tiebreaker. Still tied — sudden death playoff, captain's pick.

Still Chasing the Breakthrough

The Pelican Lake Cup started in 2020 while the Lundquist cabin was still being built. Ten guys, three rounds at Deacons Lodge, a camper in the yard, and a half-finished cabin turned into a family golf tradition with captains, drafts, uniforms, side games, and plenty of score-settling.

2020Top Guns

The Inaugural Cup

Sept 10–13 · Deacons Lodge

Erik had wanted to organize a Ryder Cup-style event for years. The first version came together during the summer the new Pelican Lake cabin was being built, with players spread between the old yellow cabin, Mark Troutman's camper, and a neighbor's guest house.
