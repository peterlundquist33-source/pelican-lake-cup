Before You Enter

What to Expect

Handicaps

A GHIN or verified handicap index is preferred. No GHIN? Your average score can be used to set a fair index. Every format applies its own allowance — see Format.

The Draft

Captains select their rosters from the registered field. Registering early helps: in 2024 the draft moved forward so team shirts could be sized and ordered in time.

The Week

Five rounds of competitive golf, an opening night dinner cooked by the Cinderella Boyz, side games, lake time, and the presentation of the Cup. See Championship Weekend.

The Pelican Lake Cup

A Ryder Cup-style showdown between family and friends. Two teams. Five formats. One trophy. Bragging rights forever.
