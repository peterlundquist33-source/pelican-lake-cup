Still chasing the breakthrough

The Pelican Lake Cup started in 2020 while the Lundquist cabin was still being built. Ten guys, three rounds at Deacons Lodge, a camper in the yard, and a half-finished cabin turned into a family golf tradition with captains, drafts, uniforms, side games, and plenty of score-settling.

2020 Top Guns

The Inaugural Cup

September 10-13, 2020 · Deacons Lodge · Cabin under construction

Erik had wanted to organize a Ryder Cup-style event for years. The first version came together during the summer the new Pelican Lake cabin was being built, with players spread between the old yellow cabin, Mark Troutman's camper, and a neighbor's guest house.

  • Captains: Erik Lundquist and Tim Valen
  • Field: 10 players
  • Format: 15 total points, 8 to win
  • Rounds: Two-man shamble, singles match play, modified alternate shot plus scramble
2021 Top Guns

Back at the Lake

September 9-12, 2021 · Whitefish, White Birch, Crosswoods

The new cabin was finished and the Cup had a proper home base. The field expanded, Peter joined the event, and the Cinderella Boyz cooking the opening night dinner became part of the tradition.

  • Captains: Erik Lundquist and Tim Valen
  • Field: 12 players
  • Saturday dinner: Norway Ridge Supper Club
  • Result: Two years, two Top Guns victories
2022 No Event

The Gap Year

The trophy stayed on John's shelf.

No Cup was held in 2022, leaving the Top Guns unbeaten and the Cinderella Boyz waiting another year for a shot at the trophy.

2023 Top Guns

The Brothers Take Over

September 13-17, 2023 · Deacons Lodge, Golden Eagle, Whitefish

John and Peter became captains, the field grew to 16, and the draft became an event of its own: Breezy Point Airport, Wednesday night cookout, lawn chairs, and self-scouting reports from every player.

  • Top Guns captain: John Lundquist
  • Cinderella Boyz captain: Peter Lundquist
  • Format: 20 total points, 10.5 to win
  • Result: Three years, three Top Guns wins
2024 Top Guns

Deacons Lodge Takeover

September 11-15, 2024 · Deacons Lodge all three rounds

The Cup stayed at Deacons for the full weekend. Teams were drafted in advance for shirt sizing, side games were added each day, and the Top Guns kept the trophy again.

  • Top Guns: John, Ray, Logan, Drew, Pete, Hunter, Jim, Scott
  • Cinderella Boyz: Peter, Jared, Dan, Nick, CJ, Christian, Tim, Erik
  • New players: Dan Christianson and Christian Massett
  • Result: Four straight for the Top Guns

Format

Five rounds. Five formats. No hiding.

Competition Rounds

Each round uses a different format to test every part of your game — from teamwork to clutch solo play. Points are awarded match-play style and aggregate across all five rounds.

1

2v2 Scramble

Both players hit, pick the best ball, repeat. The ultimate team format — lean on your partner's strengths.

Handicap: 35% of low handicap + 15% of high handicap.

2

2v2 Shamble — Combined Net

Both players hit the tee shot, pick the best drive, then each plays their own ball in. Combined net score counts.

Handicap: 60% of course handicap for each player.

3

2v2 Shamble — Best Net

Same as Round 2 — pick the best drive, play your own ball — but only the best net score of the two partners counts each hole.

Handicap: 85% of course handicap for each player.

4

Alternate Shot Scramble

Partners alternate shots on each hole. Pick the best tee shot first, then trade off from there. Communication is everything.

Handicap: 35% of combined team handicap.

5

Singles Match Play

Head to head. Hole by hole. No partner to bail you out. This is where legends are made.

Handicap: Full course handicap (strokes given based on difference).

Scoring

Match Play Points

Each round is scored match-play style. Results from all five rounds aggregate on the cumulative scoreboard. The team with the most total points at the end wins the Pelican Lake Cup.

Tiebreaker

In the event of a tie after five rounds, the result of Round 5 (Singles Match Play) serves as the tiebreaker. If still tied — sudden death playoff, captain's pick.
